Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. We don't collect your data.

Our Commitment

No Data Collection CorporateWale is designed with your privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. This website is a static, client-side application that runs entirely in your browser.

What We Don't Collect

  • Personal Information: No name, email, phone, or IP address.
  • Usage Data: No tracking of which songs you play or how you interact.
  • Cookies: We don't use tracking cookies or analytics scripts.
  • Location: We don't request or access your location.

Local Storage Only

The only data stored is on your own device using your browser's localStorage. This is used solely for:

  • Saving your selected background theme.
  • Remembering your volume and repeat mode preferences.
  • Tracking your session's song count (for the "X played" counter).

This data is never sent to a server and can be cleared at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

No Ads, No Tracking

CorporateWale contains no advertisements and integrates with no third-party tracking services. The music you play is either hosted locally (in the `audio/` folder) or linked via the official YouTube IFrame API, which respects YouTube's own privacy policies.
